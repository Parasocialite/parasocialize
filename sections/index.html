Parasocial is a relationship where the other end cannot say it back. It is not a genre - it is a shape, and it shows up in a stadium and in a court docket and in the teacher at the front of the room exactly as hard as it shows up in a fandom. Ten sections. Seven of them a fandom site would never build.
