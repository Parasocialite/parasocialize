Fans invented the word, and the fans are not what it means. 1990. Shipping. The word comes from The X-Files. A character in a particular episode was describing a romantic relationship that was not going anywhere. Ship it. Take it further than the other people involved want it to go. On a fan forum, people who were deeply serious about an author who would never reciprocate started calling themselves that. The word got attached to people who were in the room, and stayed attached to the people who were not. A million people use it about themselves every day, without checking who it was for.
