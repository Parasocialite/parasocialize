iYi episode - ELIZA
====================

THE VOICE IS YOURS TO SUPPLY, AND IT LEADS.

Drop your recordings in as  voice/00.wav ... voice/08.wav
One file per scene, numbered to match the scene order, wav format.

Then run:  python tools/episode.py eliza

Existing recordings are KEPT and the film retimes itself to them. A slower or
more deliberate read changes the scene durations by itself; nothing has to be
timed by hand. The voice leads and the picture follows.

To go back to the machine voice:  python tools/episode.py eliza --retts

This is deliberate rather than a workaround. A synthetic voice delivering a
thesis about a relationship being real is the video committing the thing it
argues against - viewers clock a synthetic voice in seconds and read it as the
tell that nobody is there.

FORMATS
  episode/eliza/scenes/        1080x1920 vertical  - TikTok, Reels, Shorts
  episode/eliza/scenes-land/   1920x1080 landscape - YouTube
  Two real compositions, not one cropped into the other.

ENCODE (needs ffmpeg. ffmpeg 9 removed -vsync; -fps_mode vfr replaces it)
  cd episode/eliza
  ffmpeg -y -f concat -safe 0 -i frames.txt -i narration.wav -fps_mode vfr \
    -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 20 -preset slow \
    -c:a aac -b:a 128k -shortest -movflags +faststart eliza.mp4
  ffmpeg -y -f concat -safe 0 -i frames-land.txt -i narration.wav -fps_mode vfr \
    -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -crf 20 -preset slow \
    -c:a aac -b:a 128k -shortest -movflags +faststart eliza-16x9.mp4
