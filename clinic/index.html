Anyone currently in the aisle

You do not need a problem. You need a number you have never actually written down. That is the whole prerequisite, and most people find out their cost score is uncomfortable the first time they say it aloud, which is why it works better in a room.

The standing rules

RULE 01

Nobody's is the weird one

I have held a room where a teacher and a nineteen-year-old were both quietly mortified by the same confession. The rule is that everyone has one and nobody's is the worst, and it is enforced by me.

RULE 02

Nobody gets diagnosed

Not by me and not in the room. I am a person with a degree in a subject adjacent to this, not a clinician, and I will say so the moment it matters. Attachment is not a disorder. Some of it is just love with a bad angle on it.

RULE 03

Nobody gets a prescription

You leave with a number, a plan, and an appointment. Mute one thing, call one person. That is the entire intervention. It is smaller than you want and it is the part that works.
