# Coverage map

Derived from a set of AI-generated notes on parasociality across media. The notes
contain no citations and a large number of factual errors, so nothing in them is
used as a source. What survives is the **structure**: a sweep across ten domains
and roughly two thousand years, showing where the phenomenon appears and through
which mechanisms.

This document keeps the structure and discards the errors. It exists to turn a
coverage map into a work queue.

---

## What the notes got wrong, and why the map is still worth keeping

The notes were judged against two different standards, which produced opposite
verdicts. As a bibliography they are worthless. As a **map of the terrain** they
are genuinely useful, because they tell you where to look before you look.

Errors worth recording so they are not reintroduced:

| Claim in the notes | Actually |
| --- | --- |
| Richardson, *Pamela*, 1882 | 1740–41 |
| Márquez, *One Hundred Years of Solitude*, 18th c. | 1967 |
| Chikamatsu Monzaemon, 19th c. | 17th c. |
| *Epic of Gilgamesh*, 11th c. | c. 1700 BCE, Old Babylonian |
| Gary Vaynerchuk born in Belarus | born Vilnius, Lithuania |
| *Survivor*, 1992 (elsewhere 2000) | US premiere 2000; 1992 is the Swedish original |
| Rob Cesternino = "Boston Rob" | two different people |

The document also corrects itself mid-page, apologises for errors, states that it
is "still under development," and ends with a slide deck containing the
placeholder "Your Name & Date." It is a transcript of a model hallucinating a
bibliography.

Two methodological faults recur and are worth naming, because the site commits to
avoiding both:

1. **Anachronism used as evidence.** The notes apply a term coined in 1956 to
   Homer and treat that as a finding. The honest move is to name the
   anachronism and argue the recurrence separately — which this site already
   does with loyalty stamps and shop counters, both far older than Horton and
   Wohl.
2. **Category collapse.** The notes list Hitler, Stalin, Jim Jones and David
   Koresh as "parasocial," so the term ends up meaning "persuaded by anyone."
   This site draws a hard line: *deliberate cultivation for coercive ends* is a
   different claim from *incidental celebrity attachment*, and only the first
   belongs under the former heading.

## The ten domains, mapped to real work

Each domain names the mechanism the notes gesture at, the entries it touches,
and where genuine literature actually exists.

### 1. Literature — epistolary form and the absent reply
**Mechanism:** a one-sided channel whose reply is withheld, leaving the
recipient's role vacant for the reader.

*Status:* **the strongest find in the project.** Goethe's *Werther* (1774)
withholds Wilhelm's letters by design; the reader becomes Wilhelm's stand-in.
Verified and cited — see `characters-move-in`.

Follow-ons: Richardson's *Pamela* (1740–41), Goethe's *Werther*, the epistolary
tradition generally, and the survival-of-Address literature.

*Note:* the notes' own literary section missed the central mechanism entirely
and instead argued that ancient epic "fostered parasocial connection" between
audience and hero, which is a claim about reception with no evidence behind it.

### 2. Literature — interiority as access
**Mechanism:** stream of consciousness, first person, unreliable narration.

*Status:* real scholarship exists and none of it is yet cited. Relevant to
`characters-move-in` and `tony-stark` (the actor/character distinction is exactly
this problem). The unreliable-narrator case is the sharpest: readers are held by
a voice they are simultaneously distrusting, which is a *different* relationship
from attachment and deserves its own entry.

### 3. Film and television — the studio system
**Mechanism:** crafted persona plus on-screen charisma, sold as intimacy.

*Status:* substantially covered already — `michael-jackson`, `garbo` if present,
the star-system material behind `cobain` and `prince`, `characters-move-in`.

Gap: the notes' claim that silent stars created attachment through expressive
acting is plausible and unverified.

### 4. Radio — the intimacy of the voice
**Mechanism:** a disembodied voice in the home, at a scheduled hour.

*Status:* **partly covered, and this domain is the strongest foundation the
project has.** Simmons (2009) found 1920s–30s listeners experienced two-way
communication through fan mail despite radio having no interactive technology, and
concluded interactivity "may depend on media content rather than media
technology." Lanckman (2020) verified fan-letter writers against census records.

Gap: `dere` (Dionne Warwick and reciprocal fan mail) is unsourced and is *the*
documented counter-example to everything else on this site — worth prioritising.

### 5. Theology — the one-sided conversation
**Mechanism:** prayer, addressed to someone who does not answer.

*Status:* **this is the most underrated domain here and it is nearly done.** The
Filipino priests study is the whole thesis in an abstract: same apparatus,
reciprocity that never arrives. Weber on charismatic authority covers
`candidacy-and-charisma`.

Gap: guru–disciple as a distinct structure from priest–congregation.

### 6. Children's media — direct address
**Mechanism:** the fourth wall broken deliberately, early and repeatedly.

*Status:* unsourced and unexamined, and ethically the most consequential domain —
the site argues for restraint here, so it should not assert about it without
evidence. `saturday-mornings` covers the scheduling history only.

### 7. Art and architecture
**Mechanism:** addressed portraiture; a viewer confronted with scale.

*Status:* **the domain I would cut.** The notes reach for Caravaggio's lighting
and neoclassical scale, which is aesthetic experience, not one-sided attachment.
Unless a specific claim can be made about a *portrait* functioning as an absent
person, this is analogy rather than subject. Recommend excluding rather than
padding.

### 8. Talk shows and news
**Mechanism:** the professional persona as companion; authority as intimacy.

*Status:* unsourced. The overlap with `the-audience` (thesis) is total, so any
citation here risks restating our own claim as a finding.

### 9. Competition and reality formats
**Mechanism:** unscripted access plus a voting mechanism — viewers become judges.

*Status:* partly covered by `reality-celebrity` (the format's origin as
unembodied surveillance) and `congregation`.

Genuine leads the notes produced, both of which are worth following:
- **The confessional booth** — a direct-address device built into the edit, the
  same mechanism as `live-commentary` applied to a game.
- **"Edgic"** — Survivor fan analytics. Fandom as quantitative modelling of
  people who do not know they are being modelled. That is `the-algorithm-decides`
  happening in a fan forum, and it is a strong entry.

The notes' season-by-season player rankings are worthless as scholarship and
should not be revived.

### 10. The abuse cases
**Mechanism:** cultivation deliberately instrumentalised.

*Status:* **already the best-sourced part of the site.** `grief-extraction`,
`deepfake-grief`, `mob-and-internet-pile`, `cancelling`, `gamergate`,
`loyalty-dark-pattern`, `the-priest`, `peak-tv` pending.

This domain is where the notes' instinct was correct and where the site should
concentrate. The claim that historical figures consciously used this is sound —
Weber, cult psychology and propaganda research all exist — but it needs real
sources, and the notes supply none.
