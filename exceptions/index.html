RECIPROCITY — THE EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions

This site is named after one-sided attachment. A corpus about that subject that could not count its own exceptions would not be measuring the thing it is named after. So here they are, including the two ways the count is probably wrong.

10of 240 entries carry a value
3reciprocated
5simulated
2marked directed

The other 230 are unlabelled on purpose. directed is the default condition of everything collected here, and marking all of them would bury the ones worth naming. An earlier draft of this page was going to be a three-bar chart; it was measured first, and the chart read as an axis that was mostly unclassified rather than one that is deliberately sparse.

3 reciprocated

The other end would be missed if it stopped. These three sit 17,020 years apart — The dog -15000 · Dionne Warwick's fan mail 1963 · Kobe Bryant 2020 — which is the argument for the whole site in one line. The exceptions are not a modern artifact.

5 simulated

Reciprocity manufactured on one side and sold as the product. They arrive in exactly two clusters: Somebody always wrote back 1930 · Entitled to the adulation of all lesser mortals 1940 · Registered by the applause meter 1945 · Live chat, two-way 2015 · The subscription tier 2015. Three in fifteen years, two in a single year, and 70 years with nothing in between.

We do not know why, and we are not going to guess. It may be a real feature of how mediated audiences work. It may be that a century of entries has never been checked against this axis at all. We think it is the second one, and the next paragraph is the evidence rather than the assurance.

The admission

8 of these 10 carry no value on the otherness axis either. Only 2 do. The reciprocity and otherness axes were filled independently and have largely not been reconciled with each other — so the silence above is at least as likely to be our bookkeeping as it is to be a feature of the world. We would rather say that here than let a clean-looking chart imply we know.

Where the exceptions are not

7 of our 15 media hold at least one. The other 8 hold none: ai, games, music, stage, television_scripted, television_unscripted, theology, visual_art. Artificial intelligence has twenty entries and not one exception on this axis.
