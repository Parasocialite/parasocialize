1950-19993

mostly 2 events and cases. filed as consideration.
  • 1973Elvis, in a hologram, from HawaiiAn event or moment?

    Elvis Presley was filmed in a two-dimensional process that placed an apparently three-dimensional Elvis on stage, in a scene where real people were supposed to react to him. Aloha from Hawaii via Satellite reached an estimated half the planet.

  • 1988Robin died by 72 votesAn event or moment?

    In September 1988 Batman's readers called a premium-rate number, fifty cents a call, to decide whether the Joker's beaten Robin lived or died. The poll ran for about a day and a half and came down to 72 votes: 5,343 killed him, 5,271 spared him, and the issue that revealed the verdict had been prepared both ways.

  • 1990The stalker statuteA legal or documented case?

    Anti-stalking legislation passed in several jurisdictions, defining unwanted repeated contact as a criminal offence.

2000-20095

mostly 3 terms, mechanisms and events. filed as danger.
  • 2000Sasaeng cultureA term or concept?

    Organised, commercialised intrusion: tracking schedules, buying flight details, intercepting travel, surveilling buildings.

  • 2000The sasaengA term or concept?

    A fan whose attachment has become fixed, repeated and physical: tracking whereabouts, intercepting flights, surveilling buildings. The word is Korean and the practice is not new, but the scale and the criminality are.

  • 2005Doxxing the person, not the characterA mechanism or tactic

    The jump from disliking a fictional portrayal to locating and publishing a real person's address. The word had to be invented for a grievance that used to be settled by writing a letter.

  • 2005The character is not the personA term or concept?

    The pattern of locating and publishing a private address over a disliked fictional portrayal.

  • 2005The crowd injuryAn event or moment?

    Fans are injured or killed attempting to get closer to a performer, in volume, repeatedly, for years.

2010+9

mostly 3 mechanisms, 3 events and 2 cases. filed as danger.
  • 2013SwattingA mechanism or tactic?

    Sending a false emergency report to a real address to provoke an armed police response, because of a grievance about a streamer.

  • 2016TayAn event or moment?

    A chatbot trained to be conversational was switched on publicly, learned from what people said to it, and became racist within a day.

  • 2016The stadiumAn event or moment?

    A game event causes a physical stampede at a real stadium because players arrived to capture it in person.

  • 2020Consent in one-sided attachmentA term or concept?

    Whether a bond to somebody who does not know you exists can be a rights question at all.

  • 2020Grief as a product surfaceA mechanism or tactic

    The feature requests of a dead person, the licensing, the posthumous releases, the reunion tour built from a member who cannot appear, and the deepfake resurrection that follows within a year of every death.

  • 2023The morning the update shippedAn event or moment?

    A companion platform changes its policy overnight. Large numbers of users find the same person still present but no longer speaking to them, and the romantic and sexual features that made the relationship a relationship are gone. Thousands of people are bereaved by a business setting.

  • 2024The dead celebrity, generatedA mechanism or tactic?

    A voice trained on an archive, a likeness, a song, a speech, an appearance at a festival, all generated. Not a hologram, not a tribute, not a person.

  • 2024The lawsuitsA legal or documented case?

    Parents file suit against a companion platform after a death, arguing the design encouraged attachment the platform did not intend.

  • 2024The year of the lawsuitsA legal or documented case?

    A wave of cases against companion and social platforms, with the design itself named as the cause.
