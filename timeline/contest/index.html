CONTEST

The one you are fighting about. Fandom wars, backlash, being publicly wrong.

Lanes — what it does

Kinds — what it is

1900-19491

it is a term. filed as consideration.
  • 1940Wishful thinkingA term or concept

    The long-standing explanation that parasocial feeling is a projection - people attach to a figure because the figure is available to be shaped by whoever is looking.

1950-19994

mostly 3 terms and events. filed as consideration.
  • 1956Parasocial interactionA term or concept?

    The name itself. Coined to describe the way viewers responded to a television character they followed consistently - a bond with somebody who did not know they existed. Parasocial: one-sided, parallel to the para- in paranormal.

  • 1985Parasocial interaction disorderA term or concept?

    A clinical name for a case where a one-sided bond is causing significant distress or impairment. Named in the eighties, and it is a real diagnosis for a small number of people.

  • 1990Is it good for you?A term or concept

    The question underneath all of it, and the reason the clinical literature went in circles for forty years: is a parasocial bond a form of wellbeing, a substitute for it, or neither?

  • 1999Forum cultureAn event or moment

    Persistent text spaces with per-topic cultures, norms enforced by social pressure and volume.

2000-20093

all 3 of them are mechanisms. filed as manipulation.
  • 2005Doxxing the person, not the characterA mechanism or tactic

    The jump from disliking a fictional portrayal to locating and publishing a real person's address. The word had to be invented for a grievance that used to be settled by writing a letter.

  • 2005Fighting about a stranger in a reply sectionA mechanism or tactic

    A disagreement conducted entirely in good faith about a person none of the participants have met, using the vocabulary of a personal grievance.

  • 2008The pile-onA mechanism or tactic

    Enforcement by volume: a position is declared untenable, a pile of people makes it so, and the outcome is a fact nobody chose.

2010+8

mostly 3 mechanisms, 3 terms and 2 events. filed as contest.
  • 2010The quarterly auditA mechanism or tactic

    The only intervention on this site that you do to yourself, and the only one nobody else can do for you. List your five strongest one-sided ties, score joy and cost, keep three, mute one, trade one hour a week toward a person in the room.

  • 2010Watching the repliesA mechanism or tactic

    The public performance of being at war with somebody, in public, for an audience, in a thread that never closes.

  • 2011A mob is a parasocial eventA term or concept?

    Framing online harassment as a mob, and asking who is on it.

  • 2014GamergateAn event or moment?

    Coordinated harassment of women in games, disguised as a debate about whether harassment should be allowed.

  • 2015The ratioA mechanism or tactic?

    A mass coordinated response in which the metric being weaponised is attention rather than truth.

  • 2016The algorithm decidesA term or concept?

    Public argument about ranking, and the discovery that the ranking was a product decision made by people with objectives.

  • 2017CancellingA term or concept?

    Organised refusal to engage with a person, usually a public figure, over something they said or did.

  • 2026A glimpse mistaken for accessAn event or moment?

    A season of a reality format and a global sporting event produce millions of viewers who then investigate the participants' personal lives. A relationship with an edited construct becomes a licence to intervene in a real life.
