CREATOR

The one who shows up every day until you know their schedule.

Lanes — what it does

Kinds — what it is

Before 01

it is a mechanism. filed as application.
  • 660 BCThe craft of the sage AdapaA mechanism or tactic

    A tablet from Nineveh written in the voice of Ashurbanipal, listing what he had been taught and by whom - a named sage he never met - and then what he did with it, which was to put the tablets in his palace for the instruction of his subjects.

1900-19491

it is a mechanism. filed as application.
  • 1930The cliffhangerA mechanism or tactic

    End the episode before the answer. Give the viewer a fixed slot, a daily rhythm, and a question with no resolution, and they will come back tomorrow at the same time for years.

1950-19991

it is a event. filed as application.
  • 1997WebcastingAn event or moment?

    Streaming video over a network, at first unbroadcast and unproduced, from a bedroom or a desk.

2000-20094

mostly 3 events and terms. filed as application.
  • 2000Transparasocial interactionA term or concept?

    The 1990s extension to live people who respond in real time - streamers, hosts, people who read your comment. The bond is still fundamentally one-sided, but the other side can now hear you.

  • 2005YouTube launchesAn event or moment?

    Uploads become trivially cheap and the audience begins to attach to individuals rather than channels.

  • 2007The phone becomes a personAn event or moment

    Roughly half a billion phone numbers become dialable, a fan can in principle hear a recorded voice, and the novelty ringtone sells the idea that a performer can be present in a pocket for about four years.

  • 2009The Bieber effectAn event or moment?

    A performer with no media infrastructure becomes a global star through a video-sharing platform alone.

2010+15

mostly 7 mechanisms, 6 events and terms. filed as manipulation.
  • 2010The attention economyA term or concept?

    The shift from selling things to selling attention, which means the commodity being bought is a person's feeling - and the seller is whoever is on the other end of it.

  • 2010Watching the repliesA mechanism or tactic

    The public performance of being at war with somebody, in public, for an audience, in a thread that never closes.

  • 2011Justin.tv becomes TwitchAn event or moment?

    Streaming gets a category, a vocabulary, a schedule, and a professional class of people who stream full time.

  • 2011Nobody answeredA mechanism or tactic?

    The schedule goes from weekly to continuous and the bond loses its shape. A serial gives you a slot and a cliffhanger; an always-on feed has no slot and no gap to miss anybody in.

  • 2013SwattingA mechanism or tactic?

    Sending a false emergency report to a real address to provoke an armed police response, because of a grievance about a streamer.

  • 2014Parasocial research on creators beginsA study or paper?

    A wave of studies measuring parasocial relationships with YouTubers, livestreamers and vloggers rather than with television hosts.

  • 2014The podcast as an intimate mediumA mechanism or tactic?

    A voice in your ear, unedited, twice a week, for years. The most parasocial habit in the English language and rarely discussed as such.

  • 2015Live chat, two-wayA mechanism or tactic

    A live stream with a visible comment feed, where the creator can read the room while the room watches itself. The first mass parasocial environment where the one-sided bond visibly, continuously, acknowledges you.

  • 2015The subscription tierA mechanism or tactic

    A monthly payment that buys recognition, and a chat in which the size of that payment is the mechanism for being noticed. Gifting, tiers, emotes, raids.

  • 2016The virtual creatorA mechanism or tactic?

    A performer with an animated persona, a persistent character and an industry-standard model where the audience pays for the character's recognition.

  • 2021The doorstepAn event or moment?

    A viewer who donated enough arrives at a creator's home believing he knows her, and the boundary is a news event.

  • 2022The talent agency that is not an agencyAn event or moment?

    A high-profile streaming collective, featuring several women who had left other setups, collapses publicly and in litigation.

  • 2024The two-year banAn event or moment?

    A platform's largest creator is suspended and reinstated. The audience experiences it as something happening to a person they know.

  • 2026Creators barred from the industry conferenceAn event or moment?

    A creator economy conference puts platform executives and working creators on one stage and turns away the audience that makes both possible.

  • 2026The creator joins the living roomAn event or moment?

    Creator posts and viewer comments are built for the television set, putting a two-way channel on the biggest screen in the house.
