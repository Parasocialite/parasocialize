  • 1438BindingA mechanism or tactic

  • 1900The coachA mechanism or tactic

  • 1900The landlordA mechanism or tactic

    A named individual with access to your home, your deposits and your address, met in person, repeatedly, under stress.

  • 1900The teacherA mechanism or tactic

1950-19999

  • 1950The local news anchorA mechanism or tactic?

    One person on your own television, in your own town, for twenty years, who is leaving and everybody is upset.

  • 1950The neighbourA mechanism or tactic

    Someone whose kitchen light you can see, whose bin day you know, and whose life you have been tracking for years without ever having asked.

  • 1950The retail clerkA mechanism or tactic

    A person who recognises you, remembers what you wanted, and has the right thing behind the counter when you come back.

  • 1955The Macy's counter

    A sales clerk who remembered. Not a recommendation engine, not a loyalty points balance - a person who noticed what you had looked at and had the right thing ready when you came back.

  • 1960The political parasocialA mechanism or tactic?

    Support for a politician structured as a personal bond rather than a policy preference, in a way that transfers poorly across candidates.

  • 1985Loneliness and local television newsA study or paper?

    A long-running body of work from Rubin and colleagues, repeatedly finding that people form parasocial bonds with news anchors, and that the strongest predictor of which anchor they bond with is not the anchor's performance.

  • 1985The counter sits betweenA mechanism or tactic

    The person who knows you is also the person who profits from you, and is paid to be there, and goes home at six. The relationship has an employer.

  • 1990Mourning a person you metA term or concept?

    A working vocabulary for grief over a public figure, drawn from bereavement practice, used without qualification.

  • 1995Clerks beat algorithmsA mechanism or tactic

    The difference between being known by somebody who was paying attention on purpose and being modelled by a system that has your history. Same information, different relationship.

2010+4

  • 2011The assistant arrivesAn event or moment?

    A voice assistant on every phone, addressing the user by name, knowing the user's calendar, and present in the home.

  • 2015The people who are supposed to be reachableA term or concept

    Attachment to somebody we are not supposed to have feelings about: the teacher, the landlord, the local news anchor, the doctor's receptionist, the AI assistant that has read everything typed. The category no fandom site will admit exists.

  • 2020Consent in one-sided attachmentA term or concept?

    Whether a bond to somebody who does not know you exists can be a rights question at all.

  • 2022The assistant with your whole life in itA mechanism or tactic?

    A system holding your calendar, your messages, your health questions and your voice, in a private conversational register.
