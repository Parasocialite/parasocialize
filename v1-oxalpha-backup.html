01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PARASOCIAL

Made by one. Aimed at many.

Enan Joe Heneghan came up in the western suburbs of Chicago — Downers Grove, class of the '90s — then managed Blockbuster stores across three states, earned an M.A., and landed, eventually and delightedly, in front of classrooms in Australia.

The through-line is attention: essays on medieval binding rituals, novels about industries that harvest your sleeping mind, albums built inside dead machines, podcasts about the strange gravity between performer and audience.

Everything on this frequency is made by one person and pointed at you specifically. Yes, you.
