Top 8: The Leader Who Knows Your Name

A leader is a figure with an office and an audience, and the two are easy to confuse. These are the moments the confusion was used.

The leader is a figure with an office and an audience. Ranked by how much of the relationship is the office talking.

Political attachment is the parasocial bond with a budget and a police force. The eight below are the record of what happens when the figure who addresses you from a screen also has the power to change the room you are watching from.

  1. 1

    Your face is too well known

    Visual art · 1930

    A dentist and a painter's sister were made into a national type on purpose, and one of them denied it for five years.

  2. 2

    The fan club

    Literature · 1950

    A formal organisation with membership, dues, an elected president, a newsletter and rules. Not a spontaneous thing that happened to fans - a structure somebody built for them and that fans then ran themselves.

    Read the entry →
  3. 3

    The political parasocial

    History · 1960

    Support for a politician structured as a personal bond rather than a policy preference, in a way that transfers poorly across candidates.

    Read the entry →
  4. 4

    The icon answers nothing

    Visual art / Theology · 787

    In 787 the Second Council of Nicaea ended the first phase of the Byzantine iconoclastic dispute and defined what an icon was for: the honour paid to the image passes to the figure it shows.

    Read the entry →
  5. 5

    The face he painted for the army was his own

    Visual art · 1917

    James Montgomery Flagg drew the most famous recruiting poster in the world and modelled the figure from a mirror - so the finger points at you with the artist's own face.

    Read the entry →
  6. 6

    As if he had joined them in their living rooms

    Radio · 1933

    Franklin Roosevelt talked to the radio as if the listener were already in the room, and he did it often enough between March 1933 and June 1944 that the habit became a form - one he did not name.

    Read the entry →
  7. 7

    Think of nothing but the guru

    Theology · 1650

    Guru devotion asks a disciple to hold one teacher in mind without pause - moving, walking, sleeping, sitting, happy or miserable - and the Tibetan tradition makes it indispensable to the path rather than optional.

    Read the entry →
  8. 8

    The narrator is Tyler Durden, and he is not told

    Film · 1999

    Two hours of a man telling you about his best friend. The friend does not exist, the man telling you does not know it, and you are being addressed by somebody who is also being lied to.
