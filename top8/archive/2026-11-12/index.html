Parasocialize Top 8 — November 12, 2026

The eight that ran that day, frozen as published. Later builds do not revise a day.

← 2026-11-08 Every day →
  1. 01

    Companion 1958

    The tape could not answer

    A man on his sixty-ninth birthday plays a recording he made at thirty-nine, and has the conversation the tape cannot have back.

  2. 02

    Character 1904

    If you believe, clap your hands

    The play that asks the whole audience to save a dying fairy by clapping - and has received the same answer, in the same room, for more than a century.

  3. 03

    Persona 1891

    The Divine Sarah

    Sarah Bernhardt toured more like a head of state than a player: when she sailed for New York in 1891 the crossing was covered the way a royal visit is, and 'Divine Sarah' was by then the whole public's phrase for her.

  4. 04

    Cosmology 1976

    The audience became the show

    Launched as a small London stage musical in 1973, filmed in 1975, and from 1976 screened at midnight before a room that shouted back: The Rocky Horror Picture Show ran while its audience performed it, wearing the costumes, speaking the lines, improvising replies.

  5. 05

    Cosmology 1985

    Vote for the murderer

    A musical of Dickens' unfinished novel that stops where the novel stops and turns the cliffhanger over to the seats: the audience votes who murdered Edwin Drood, who the disguised Dick Datchery is and which two characters end up together, and the cast performs the chosen ending that night.

  6. 06

    Persona 2013

    A slightly more awkward version of himself

    A comedian who stages real situations for people who have not read the script, and performs a slightly more awkward version of himself inside them - the manufacturing is the show, and the audience has been told so from the beginning.

  7. 07

    Character 1758

    The wall exists to keep you out

    Diderot invented the wall in 1758 for the purpose of stopping the audience noticing.
