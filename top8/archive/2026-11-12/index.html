  1. 01

    Institution 2002

    The forum that scored the edit

    Edgic - editing plus logic - is the practice of grading every contestant's screen presence week by week: a category (invisible, under the radar, middle of the road, complex personality, over the top), a tone and a visibility score, charted in public so a community can read what the production is doing and name the winner before the merge.

  2. 02

    Institution 1992

    The confessional that became a convention

    In 1992 MTV's The Real World gave reality television its least-questioned convention: a person alone in a room, speaking to a camera about what everyone just saw.

  3. 03

    Companion 1947

    Say, kids, what time is it?

    The first television character to be answered by name.

  4. 04

    Predator 2026

    A glimpse mistaken for access

    A season of a reality format and a global sporting event produce millions of viewers who then investigate the participants' personal lives.

  5. 05

    Persona 1971

    The figure you buy

    A fictional figure whose NAME is the product: the bond is with a person who was invented so that the relationship can never fail.

  6. 06

    Persona 1997

    Webcasting

    Streaming video over a network, at first unbroadcast and unproduced, from a bedroom or a desk.

  7. 07

    Institution 2005

    YouTube launches

    Uploads become trivially cheap and the audience begins to attach to individuals rather than channels.

  8. 08

    Officer 1950

    The local news anchor

    One person on your own television, in your own town, for twenty years, who is leaving and everybody is upset.
