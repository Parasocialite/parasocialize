Parasocialize Top 8 — November 3, 2026

The eight that ran that day, frozen as published. Later builds do not revise a day.

← 2026-11-022026-11-04 → Every day →
  1. 01

    Persona 1921

    They were not his, and they had never been

    A screen persona called the Latin Lover, and a death at thirty-one that produced public mourning on a scale nothing before it had managed - including a woman claiming an engagement he had never mentioned to anyone.

  2. 02

    Institution 2020

    Grief as a product surface

    The feature requests of a dead person, the licensing, the posthumous releases, the reunion tour built from a member who cannot appear, and the deepfake resurrection that follows within a year of every death.

  3. 03

    Character 1956

    He did not ask to be buried as Dracula

    The most reproduced image in the horror canon - a man interred in his character's cape - is true, and the story that he chose it is false.

  4. 04

    Divine -250

    The wax masks in the hall

    A Roman aristocrat had a wax mask of his own face taken while he was alive - usually in his thirties or forties, on taking office - and it was kept in a cupboard in the entrance hall beside a painted family tree and a label listing his offices.

  5. 05

    Character 2019

    The ensemble farewell

    A film that functioned as a funeral for eleven characters and was marketed as a collective event.

  6. 06

    Persona 2004

    Michael Jackson

    Public mourning in a way that produced its own genre of documentary and speculation.

  7. 07

    Character 1994

    The character death

    Killing a popular character for narrative effect, and the organised reaction that follows.

  8. 08

    Divine -34000

    Beads, and one bull

    At Sunghir on the Russian plain, thirty-four thousand years ago, three burials were lavish with beads and ochre and one was not treated that way at all.
