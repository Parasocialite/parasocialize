Top 8: The Dead Star Still Working

The death is the moment the one-sided bond becomes public property. Ranked by how completely the record shows the mechanism.

Nobody is buried alone any more. The eight below are the ways a dead figure keeps working: the funeral that belonged to the audience, the estate that licenses the voice, the grave that becomes a destination. None of them requires the dead person to have agreed to any of it.

  1. 1

    They were not his, and they had never been

    Film · 1921

    A screen persona called the Latin Lover, and a death at thirty-one that produced public mourning on a scale nothing before it had managed - including a woman claiming an engagement he had never mentioned to anyone.

    Grief as a product surface

    Artificial intelligence · 2020

    The feature requests of a dead person, the licensing, the posthumous releases, the reunion tour built from a member who cannot appear, and the deepfake resurrection that follows within a year of every death.

    Read the entry →
  3. 3

    He did not ask to be buried as Dracula

    Film · 1956

    The most reproduced image in the horror canon - a man interred in his character's cape - is true, and the story that he chose it is false. His mother chose it.

    Read the entry →
  4. 4

    The wax masks in the hall

    History · 250 BC–550

    A Roman aristocrat had a wax mask of his own face taken while he was alive - usually in his thirties or forties, on taking office - and it was kept in a cupboard in the entrance hall beside a painted family tree and a label listing his offi.

    The ensemble farewell

    Film · 2019

    A film that functioned as a funeral for eleven characters and was marketed as a collective event.

    Beads, and one bull

    History · 34000 BC
