Parasocialize Top 8 — November 27, 2026

The eight that ran that day, frozen as published. Later builds do not revise a day.

← 2026-11-26 Every day →
  1. 01

    Character 1930

    Somebody always wrote back

    Fifty-four years of letters to a fictional detective, answered by an office that had chosen not to have a face.

  2. 02

    Persona 1927

    The girl the studio built

    Paramount signed Clara Bow in 1926 and released six of her pictures in 1927, one of which - It - put a novelist's definition of 'It' onto a face and left the language with the phrase 'It Girl'.

  3. 03

    Companion 1922

    Most faithfully, Rin Tin Tin

    A German Shepherd found on a battlefield became one of the biggest silent-film stars, and the fan mail that arrived for him was answered with a glossy portrait signed by a paw.

  4. 04

    Persona 1924

    The radio church of Sister Aimee

    Her church went on the air on 6 February 1924: KFSG - 'Kall Foursquare Gospel' - broadcasting at 500 watts from Angelus Temple, listener-supported one day at a time.

  5. 05

    Institution 1962

    The reply that was printed

    From 1962 Stan Lee ran fan mail in the back of The Fantastic Four and answered it in print in his own voice, so a reader could see their name answered on a page.

  6. 06

    Institution 1921

    Cordially yours, Betty Crocker

    In 1921 a flour company's advertising department needed one woman's name for the kitchen advice its all-female home-service staff was already writing.

  7. 07

    Institution 1911

    Thousands of letters, and a handful printed

    The first movie fan magazine appeared in February 1911, because by 1910 the letters were arriving faster than the studios could absorb them.

  8. 08

    Companion 1942

    I hope the feeling is mutual

    Two years of letters to a listener who could not answer, written by a thirteen-year-old who said out loud that she hoped the feeling was mutual.
