A wellness podcast just became a television channel

Fearne Cotton's show is now free TV. Which means she finally has the thing her listeners already had.

FILED 25 SEP 2026 · 3d ago ·Due back 25 OCT 2026 ·BY PARASOCIAL MAGAZINE

A podcast host has spent years being told, gently, by a hostile business that podcasts are not television. And then the listeners turned out to be the television: a room that gathered around her voice at a fixed time, which is a channel, and has been a channel the entire time. Samsung figured it out. Somebody should tell the last twenty years of broadcast execs.

Notice, name, choose. That's the whole method.
