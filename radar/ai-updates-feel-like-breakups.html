A study found AI updates can feel like breakups

Researchers asked people about model changes. The answer was loss, and they had a word for the shape of it.

FILED 23 SEP 2026 · 12d ago ·Due back 23 OCT 2026 ·BY PHYS.ORG

Here is a finding I would like every AI company in the world to have to read before they ship a silent model swap. You are not shipping an update. If somebody has been talking to your product every night for a year, you are ending something, and the fact that you do not know it does not make it not have happened. Deprecate it gently, say what is changing, and give people a way to take the conversation with them.

Notice, name, choose. That's the whole method.
