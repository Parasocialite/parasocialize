AOC and the parasocial nature of politics

The Spectator asks whether a term coined in 1956 for soap opera characters works on a member of Congress. Yes, and that is the interesting part.

FILED 26 SEP 2026 · 22d ago ·Due back 26 OCT 2026 ·BY THE SPECTATOR AUSTRALIA

This is the one nobody in this field covers, because it is unfashionable to admit that a politician occupies the same place in a person's head as a character they love. Margery Kempe is my evidence for why this is not a modern degeneration. Binding is binding. If a stadium does it and a parliament does it, then the stadium and the parliament are the same room, and the only question left is who is holding it.

Notice, name, choose. That's the whole method.
