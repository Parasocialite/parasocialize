The platform's big creator conference banned fans

VidSummit put a Twitch CEO and creators on stage together, and turned away the people who made it possible.

Every industry has the one event where the people who actually pay are not in the room, and apparently this one has a name badge for it. I have worked retail. We had a back-of-house night. Everyone laughed about it and everyone showed up. The whole economy of this runs on a relationship with somebody watching, and the conference for that relationship decided the watchers were a distraction.

And so do people who like novelty and good conversation, which is most of us and a good deal less exotic than the discourse assumes.
