Why fiction sticks when nothing else does

Shipping, kinning, comfort watches, and the safest place on earth to find out what you actually feel.

Fictional bonds are the well-studied ones because they are the only ones with a fire exit. Dreamarched is a whole descent through that, into an architecture you keep going back to. The only line worth drawing is where the room starts making house rules, and you can always feel that coming before you can explain it.
