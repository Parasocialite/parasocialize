An event or moment · 1991 · Television

The NEST letter

A child writes to a magazine telling them a fictional brand of baby food is real. The NEST never appears. The letter is never answered. The episode ends.

ApplicationConsiderationtelevision scripted

The single most parasocial thing ever put on television, and it is a joke about wanting the writers to confirm that a fictional place is real. An eight-year-old asking to be told the world is real, dressed as a gag about a cereal. Everything on this site happens because a child did not accept the terms.

Application. One-sided attachment put to work on purpose. Creator economies, community, fandom as infrastructure, and the things that genuinely help people. Not automatically good - a tool with an obvious use is a tool that will also get a bad one.

Consideration. The part where somebody stops and thinks. The research, the diagnosis, the ethics, the lawsuits, the regulators. Roughly fifty years of arguing with itself about whether this is a relationship.
