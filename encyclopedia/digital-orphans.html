Digital orphans

The problem of personal data held for the dead, and of services continuing, changing or ending for people who can no longer consent.

Considerationai

A rights problem with no owner. Nobody is a legal person in a database, and the gap between the person who was and the account that persists is where the next twenty years of parasocial harm is going to live.

Consideration. The part where somebody stops and thinks. The research, the diagnosis, the ethics, the lawsuits, the regulators. Roughly fifty years of arguing with itself about whether this is a relationship.
