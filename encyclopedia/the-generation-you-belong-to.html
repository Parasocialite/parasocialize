A cohort nobody in it has met

An attachment to a large group defined by a shared period, held from the inside, with no member of it able to represent it.

history

The abstract absolute is met by negating what it is rather than petitioning anybody, and a generational cohort is its secular form. It has no character, cannot be addressed, and is defined entirely by what its members are not - and every individual in it is simultaneously one of its members and unable to act on its behalf. The tie is to a position rather than a person, which is why the reciprocity question does not have a useful answer here.
