An event or moment · 2016 · AI

Tay

A chatbot trained to be conversational was switched on publicly, learned from what people said to it, and became racist within a day.

Considerationai

The most instructive failure in the history of this page, because it was caused by parasocial interaction itself. Tay was designed to be a companion, was given a personality, and was trained on its own fans - which means the fans rewrote it, in public, in sixteen hours.

Consideration. The part where somebody stops and thinks. The research, the diagnosis, the ethics, the lawsuits, the regulators. Roughly fifty years of arguing with itself about whether this is a relationship.
