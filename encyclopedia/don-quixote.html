A person · 1605 · Early modern

Don Quixote

An old man who reads so many books about chivalry that he goes out to live as a knight, against all evidence.

Applicationliterature

The founding case of a reader who mistakes a book for a world and then lives in it anyway. The whole book's plot is the argument that a person can be entirely sincere about a bond with something that is not there, and the reader is not asked to think less of him for it.

Application. One-sided attachment put to work on purpose. Creator economies, community, fandom as infrastructure, and the things that genuinely help people. Not automatically good - a tool with an obvious use is a tool that will also get a bad one.
