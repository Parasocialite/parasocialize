Fighting about a stranger in a reply section

A disagreement conducted entirely in good faith about a person none of the participants have met, using the vocabulary of a personal grievance.

ManipulationConsiderationtechnology

A parasocial relationship conducted in public with a complete stranger, and the argument is never about the stranger. The people with the strongest feelings are the most likely to be drawn in, which is the same property that makes fandoms work.

Manipulation. One-sided attachment engineered for somebody else's benefit. Paid intimacy, parasocial persuasion, sasaeng, grief extraction, the streak. The lane that pays the bills and the lane that causes the most harm, frequently at the same time.

Consideration. The part where somebody stops and thinks. The research, the diagnosis, the ethics, the lawsuits, the regulators. Roughly fifty years of arguing with itself about whether this is a relationship.
