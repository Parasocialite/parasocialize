The lobby

A fan harmed in an attempt to breach a celebrity's home, and a petition that followed.

The moment international fandom organised as an advocacy body, and the moment it was clear that a fandom can be a political actor with a legal strategy. The petition was both a moral position and an extremely effective piece of marketing for the industry.
