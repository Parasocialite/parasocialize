A person · 1774 · Enlightenment

Werther

The young man of Goethe's novel who reads too much, loves a woman who does not love him back, and kills himself, in a book that made that behaviour fashionable across Europe for a decade.

Applicationliterature

The prototype of the reader who goes looking for a character in order to feel something, and then brings the feeling home. Published in 1774; within two years the resulting condition had a name, Wertherfieber, and ministers of religion were issuing warnings about it.

Application. One-sided attachment put to work on purpose. Creator economies, community, fandom as infrastructure, and the things that genuinely help people. Not automatically good - a tool with an obvious use is a tool that will also get a bad one.
