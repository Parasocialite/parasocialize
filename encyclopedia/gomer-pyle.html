A mechanism or tactic · 1960 · Television

The sitcom family

A recurring family, played by the same cast, five nights a week, for years, in a house you could draw from memory.

Applicationtelevision scripted

Attachment to a fictional household, watched nightly, in the pre-internet equivalent of a comfort rewatch. Research on sitcom parasocial attachment found the same pattern as soaps: it is the accumulation, not the plot.

Application. One-sided attachment put to work on purpose. Creator economies, community, fandom as infrastructure, and the things that genuinely help people. Not automatically good - a tool with an obvious use is a tool that will also get a bad one.
