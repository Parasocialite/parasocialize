A mechanism or tactic · 1950 · Suburbia

The neighbour

Someone whose kitchen light you can see, whose bin day you know, and whose life you have been tracking for years without ever having asked.

Applicationhistory

A parasocial bond with somebody who is physically present and therefore, apparently, cannot be the subject of one. The reasoning is that proximity disqualifies attachment, which is exactly backwards and is why Section 07 was empty for so long.

Application. One-sided attachment put to work on purpose. Creator economies, community, fandom as infrastructure, and the things that genuinely help people. Not automatically good - a tool with an obvious use is a tool that will also get a bad one.
